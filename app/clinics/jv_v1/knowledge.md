## Services

### Initial Assessment — Musculoskeletal (MSK)
**For:** New patients | **Length:** 40 minutes | **In-clinic:** £52 | **Home visit:** £80

A thorough physiotherapy assessment of your musculoskeletal condition, leading to an accurate diagnosis and a personalised treatment plan. Marcus will take a full history, carry out a physical examination, and agree next steps with you.

**What to wear:** Comfortable, loose clothing that allows easy access to the area being assessed.

---

### MSK Treatment Session (Follow-up)
**For:** Returning patients | **Length:** 30 minutes | **In-clinic:** £46 | **Remote:** £40 | **Home visit:** £80

A focused follow-up session to continue your rehabilitation, deliver hands-on manual therapy, and progress your exercise programme. Tailored to your current stage of recovery.

**What to bring:** Any relevant scan reports or GP letters if you have them.

---

### Virtual Appointment
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 30 minutes | **Price:** £40

A video or phone consultation with Marcus — ideal for those outside Bolton and Manchester, or for review appointments that do not require hands-on treatment. Available to patients anywhere in the UK or worldwide. A video link is sent by email before the appointment.

---

### Acupuncture
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 30 minutes | **In-clinic:** £48 | **Home visit:** £70 | **Package:** 6 sessions £250

Safe, evidence-based treatment using fine sterile needles inserted at specific body points to relieve pain, reduce tension, improve circulation, and promote healing. Delivered by Marcus, who is qualified to provide acupuncture as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.

**What to wear:** Loose clothing. Avoid heavy meals immediately before the session.

---

### Sports Massage
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 30 or 60 minutes | **30 mins:** £40 | **60 mins:** £55 | **In-clinic only**

Specialist hands-on massage techniques to alleviate muscle tension, improve flexibility, support injury recovery, and enhance athletic performance. Tailored to your specific needs — whether you need deep tissue work, relaxation, or recovery support.

**What to wear:** Shorts or a vest if the lower body or back is being treated.

---

### Neurological Physiotherapy
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 60 minutes

Specialist physiotherapy for neurological conditions including multiple sclerosis, stroke, functional neurological disorder (FND), and vestibular conditions. **For:** Returning patients | **Length:** 45 minutes | **Price:** £55 | **Outdoor, Bolton area**

End-stage rehabilitation for sporting injuries, carried out outdoors with a focus on enhancing performance and safely returning to play. Ideal for athletes who are near full recovery and need sport-specific conditioning before returning to training or competition.

## Insurance protocol
Joint Venture Physiotherapy accepts private health insurance referrals, including Bupa.

**AI protocol when caller mentions insurance:**
1. Confirm private healthcare referrals are accepted.
2. Tell the caller they will need to get a pre-authorisation code from their insurer.
3. Advise them to confirm their eligibility and cover before the first appointment.
4. Offer to book provisionally while they obtain the code.
5. Take note of the insurer name for the booking.

**Do not say:** that the clinic cannot help; that insurance is not accepted; that they should claim back themselves. JV *does* accept insurance — the caller just needs the pre-auth code first.

## Medical boundaries (non-negotiable)
- Provide information, not diagnosis
- Do not give specific medication advice or prognosis timescales
- For symptoms suggesting emergency (e.g. chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke-like symptoms): advise calling 999 or going to A&E
- For clinical questions: defer to Marcus at the appointment
