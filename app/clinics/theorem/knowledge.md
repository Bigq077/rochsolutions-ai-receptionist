## Services

### Physiotherapy Assessment
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 50 minutes

Our physiotherapy assessment is a thorough, holistic first appointment lasting around 50 minutes to an hour. Rather than just looking at the physical injury, we take the whole person into account — evaluating physical mobility and strength alongside emotional well-being through a psychotherapeutic lens.

During the session, the physiotherapist will identify the injury or issue, then work collaboratively with the patient to build a tailored treatment plan. This plan can include rehabilitation exercises, manual therapy, and soft tissue work. Where appropriate, we can also incorporate more advanced interventions such as shockwave therapy, acupuncture, or Class IV laser therapy — ensuring a personalised, comprehensive path to recovery or long-term management.

**What to bring / wear:** Shorts or loose clothing are helpful if you can manage it, but don't worry if not — we'll work with whatever you've got on.

---

### Physiotherapy Follow-Up Session
**For:** Returning patients | **Length:** 50 minutes

Follow-up sessions are where the real progress happens. Each session is a collaborative checkpoint — we track how things are improving, re-evaluate mobility, strength, and overall well-being, and adjust the treatment plan as needed.

Any interventions from the initial assessment — exercises, manual therapy, advanced techniques — are fine-tuned to make sure they're still the right approach for where the patient is now. The aim is steady, supported progress rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.

If at any point it becomes clear that specialist input is needed — such as a GP, surgeon, or advanced imaging like MRI or ultrasound — the team will facilitate that referral and support the patient's onward care.

---

### Prescribing
**For:** Existing patients | **Length:** 50 minutes

Our physiotherapists are qualified prescribers, which means that where appropriate they can prescribe medication as part of a patient's care — for example, analgesia to help manage pain during recovery. This is always done with safety and effectiveness as the priority, and it forms part of the broader treatment plan rather than a standalone appointment.

---

### Remedial Rehabilitation
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 50 minutes

Alongside our physiotherapists, Theorem has expert rehabilitation instructors who guide ongoing recovery. In many cases, patients will see both their physiotherapist and a rehabilitation instructor working in tandem — a coordinated approach that delivers better, faster results than either discipline alone.

This is particularly useful when a patient needs structured, progressive exercise-based recovery alongside hands-on physiotherapy treatment.

---

### Shockwave Therapy
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** delivered within a 50-minute session

Shockwave therapy uses targeted sound waves to stimulate healing in tissues, reduce pain, and promote recovery — particularly effective for chronic conditions such as tendinopathies (e.g. Achilles tendon, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow) and calcific shoulder problems.

It's a non-invasive treatment that works by triggering the body's natural repair response in areas where tissue has become stuck or chronic. A specialist equipment surcharge of £45 applies when used.

---

### Class IV Laser Therapy
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** delivered within a 50-minute session

Class IV laser therapy is a cutting-edge, non-invasive treatment that uses powerful laser light to penetrate deep into tissue. It works to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and accelerate tissue repair — particularly useful for conditions where deeper healing is needed, such as joint pain, muscle injuries, and nerve pain.

Unlike lower-power laser devices, Class IV lasers reach deep enough to have a meaningful therapeutic effect on structures beneath the skin. A specialist equipment surcharge of £45 applies when used.

---

### Acupuncture
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** delivered within a 50-minute session

Acupuncture is an ancient practice in which fine needles are placed at specific points on the body to help balance and stimulate the flow of energy — known as Qi. By restoring this natural flow, it can promote healing, reduce pain, ease muscle tension, and improve overall well-being.

At Theorem, acupuncture is offered as part of a broader treatment plan rather than in isolation, and is used where the physiotherapist feels it will complement the patient's recovery.

---

### Psychotherapy
**For:** New and returning patients | **Length:** 50 minutes | **Price:** £85 | **Available:** Alcester only

Our psychotherapy service provides a safe, confidential space for clients to explore their thoughts and emotions. Using techniques that can include hypnotherapy and spiritual healing, sessions focus on reducing stress, improving mental well-being, and building coping strategies — helping clients navigate life's challenges and find a healthier balance.

This service reflects Theorem's belief that physical and emotional health are deeply connected. Many patients find that addressing the psychological side of an injury or chronic condition significantly accelerates their overall recovery.
