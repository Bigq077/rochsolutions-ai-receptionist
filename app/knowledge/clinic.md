# RochSolutions Clinic — Knowledge Base

RochSolutions Clinic is a private physiotherapy and musculoskeletal (MSK) clinic providing assessment, treatment, and rehabilitation for a wide range of injuries and conditions. All treatment plans are personalised following a clinical assessment by a qualified physiotherapist.

---

## Initial physiotherapy assessment

An initial physiotherapy assessment is the first appointment with the clinician.  
During this session, the physiotherapist will take a detailed history, discuss your symptoms and goals, and carry out a physical examination to understand what may be contributing to the issue.

This typically includes:
- Discussion of your symptoms, medical history, and lifestyle
- Assessment of movement, strength, flexibility, and posture
- Explanation of findings in clear, simple terms
- Initial hands-on treatment where appropriate
- Advice on exercises, activity modification, and next steps

Please wear comfortable clothing that allows the area being assessed to be examined easily. Any relevant scans, reports, or referral letters are helpful but not always required.

---

## Follow-up physiotherapy appointments

Follow-up appointments build on the initial assessment and focus on ongoing treatment and progress.  
These sessions may include hands-on therapy, exercise progression, movement retraining, and education to support recovery and reduce the risk of recurrence.

The physiotherapist will regularly reassess symptoms and adapt the treatment plan based on how you are responding.

---

## Sports massage

Sports massage focuses on soft tissue techniques to help reduce muscle tension, improve movement, and support recovery.  
It can be helpful for people who are physically active, train regularly, or experience muscular tightness from work or daily activities.

Sports massage may be used:
- Alongside physiotherapy treatment
- As part of injury prevention or recovery
- To help manage muscle soreness or stiffness

Your therapist will always discuss suitability before treatment.

---

## Shockwave therapy

Shockwave therapy uses targeted acoustic waves to stimulate tissue healing in certain persistent tendon and soft tissue conditions.  
It is often considered when symptoms have not improved with other conservative treatments.

Shockwave therapy is not suitable for everyone, and suitability is always assessed by the clinician before starting treatment.  
If appropriate, the physiotherapist will explain:
- What the treatment involves
- How many sessions may be recommended
- What to expect during and after treatment

---

## Rehabilitation and strength programmes

Rehabilitation programmes are designed to help patients return to normal daily activities, sport, or work safely and confidently.  
These programmes may include:
- Progressive strengthening exercises
- Movement and control work
- Functional rehabilitation tailored to individual goals

Programmes are adjusted over time based on progress and feedback.

---

## Conditions commonly treated

RochSolutions Clinic commonly sees patients for:
- Back and neck pain
- Shoulder, knee, hip, and ankle pain
- Sports injuries
- Tendon-related conditions
- Post-operative rehabilitation
- Work-related or postural issues

If you’re unsure whether your issue is suitable, the clinic can advise.

## Do I need a referral?

A GP or consultant referral is not usually required for private physiotherapy appointments.  
However, some insurance providers may require a referral depending on your policy.

If you’re planning to use insurance, it’s best to check directly with your provider.

## Insurance and payment

RochSolutions Clinic works with several major health insurance providers.  
Self-pay appointments are also available.

If using insurance:
- Payment and authorisation requirements vary by provider
- The clinic can provide receipts or invoices where needed
- Coverage depends on your individual policy

If you’re unsure, the clinic can help clarify next steps.

## What should I bring to my appointment?

For your appointment, please bring:
- Comfortable clothing
- Any relevant scans, reports, or letters
- Your insurance details if applicable

Arriving a few minutes early can help ensure the appointment starts on time.

## How many sessions will I need?
