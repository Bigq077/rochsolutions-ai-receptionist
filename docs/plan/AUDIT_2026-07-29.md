Same day, different calls, contradictory answers:

| Day | One call was told | Another call was offered |
|---|---|---|
| Wed 5 Aug | `CA7d424b2c`: *"quarter past six is the **latest** on the Wednesday"*, *"there's **nothing after** that"* | `CA486d6cb2`: **quarter to eight** and **half past eight** |
| Thu 6 Aug | `CAfbe9e572`: *"half past four and quarter past five — **that's everything available that day**"* | `CA16e8c6e7`: **six slots** including quarter past eight |

being told a slot they wanted did not exist — when it did.

**This converts a cosmetic readout fix into lost bookings.**
