## Step 3 — N2 · `dfc8b914`, expected to PASS

Say this **exactly**, wrong grammar and all — the wrong word is the defect:

> **"As close as possible as 12, please."**

**Expect:** **ten past twelve** is among the times she now reads back.

**The tell.** `"as"` was not a preposition, so the phrase parsed to *no* time at
all and the pin had nothing to pin. Verified offline against this build:
`requested_clock_times("as close as possible as 12") -> ['12:00']`.

❌ **FAIL** = she reads times and 12:10 is not one of them.

**Second half of N2, if the call is going well:**

> **"Do you have around 12 am?"**

**Expect:** a midday time. `"12 am"` used to read as `00:00` — never bookable —
and blank the text so the looser arm never ran. It now keeps both:
`['00:00', '12:00']`, and the day's real times pick between them.
