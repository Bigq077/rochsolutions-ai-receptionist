## Step 0 — The divert (do this before Step 3)

This is what sends his callers to Susie in the first place. Once. Not per call.

**On his business phone (+44 7586 605462), he dials:**

```
*21*07367002651#
```

Marcus is on **SMARTY**, and that is SMARTY's own published code — a **single**
star. If his handset errors, the Three version is the fallback (SMARTY runs on
Three's network):

```
**21*+447367002651#
```

- Check it worked: `*#21#` — it reads the forwarding number back
- Turn it off again: `#21#`

**It must be "all calls", not "when unanswered."** These codes are the all-calls
version. If he sets it to divert only on no-answer, his network rings his desk
first and we end up with two lots of ringing stacked on top of each other.

**His Susie SIM must have NO divert on it.** He's confirmed this. It's the phone
we ring — a divert on it points straight back at us.

## Step 2 — He texts `OFF`

He texts the single word **OFF**.

Back comes: **"Front desk mode on — calls will ring your phone first, and I'll
pick up anything you miss. Back to normal at midnight, or text ON."**

Say: *"Notice she tells you what's happening now, not just 'done'. You never

## Step 3 — You ring him. He lets it ring out.

**You** call his business number from **your** phone. Not him — his phone has
to be free to ring.

Tell him before you dial: **"Let it ring. Don't answer."**

What he'll see and hear:
1. His phone rings
2. A short message in his ear: *"Business call, from oh seven..."* — and it
   reads out your number
3. He does nothing
4. After about 12 seconds, Susie picks up and you hear her greeting

Say: *"That's the important bit. You missed it, and nobody was lost."*

## Step 4 — You ring again. This time he presses 1.

Tell him: **"Same again, but press 1 while you hear the message."**

He presses 1. You're talking to each other. Susie stays quiet.

Say: *"Press 1 and it's your call. Ignore it and it's hers. That's the whole

## Step 5 — He texts `ON`

Back comes: **"Back on — I'm answering all calls now."**

Ring once more. Susie answers straight away, no ringing on his phone.

**Leave it here. This is the state he goes home in.**

## Four things to tell him before you hang up

1. **Three words only: OFF, ON, STATUS.** On their own in the message. "Turn it
   off please" will not work — it just reaches him as a normal text. That's on
   purpose, so a patient can't flip his phones by accident.
2. **Never text STOP.** That's the phone network's own unsubscribe word, and it
   will block his texts from Susie. Use OFF.
3. **It resets itself at midnight.** If he forgets to text ON, the morning is
   normal anyway.
4. **Both his numbers work** — the Susie SIM and his main business number. Only
   his numbers. Nobody else's.

## If something goes wrong

Stay calm and say the line. Nothing on this list is a broken phone line.

| What happens | Say this |
|---|---|
| No reply to his text | *"I'll get that confirmed for you today."* Stop the demo. |
| Reply says *"Sorry — I couldn't change that just now"* | *"That's her refusing to change something she can't confirm — she'd rather tell you than guess."* It's a real safety feature. Stop the demo. |
| His phone doesn't ring on the test call | *"Let me check the divert on your side after this."* |
| Susie doesn't pick up after he ignores it | Get him to text **ON**, then ring again to show normal service. |
| Anything else odd | **Text ON, ring once, show Susie answering.** That's always the recovery. |

## Small things that stop you tripping up

- Put your own phone on **speaker** so he can hear Susie's side too.
- **Silence your laptop notifications** before you start.
- Have **+44 7367 002651** already saved and ready to dial.
- Don't demo booking, cancelling or anything else in the same breath. **One
  idea per demo.** This one is: *you can take your phone back whenever you want.*
- If you make a **test booking** at any point, cancel it by **calling Susie**,
  not by deleting it from the diary — deleting leaves the reminder texts queued.
- If a **real patient** rings while the switch is OFF, that's fine. His phone
  rings, exactly as designed.
