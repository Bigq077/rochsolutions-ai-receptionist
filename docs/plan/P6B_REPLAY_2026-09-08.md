## The measurement

`accepted_slot_is_named_in` decides whether Gate 5 suppresses the deterministic
slot offer and speaks the model instead. It is the highest-consequence boolean
in the slot layer, and both ways of getting it wrong have abandoned a real call:

* **wrong True** — the caller sits through a list they did not ask for
  (`CA4215ab7f`, 16.1 s, abandoned);
* **wrong False** — the model's correct confirmation is discarded and the list
  is read a second time (`CA5a126fe4`, the call P6b was written for).

`replay_slot_decisions.py` could not cover it: everything it scores takes
CALLER text, and this takes the MODEL's.

## A defect this turned up, and why it is NOT open

The Wednesday→Tuesday shape above looks like a live P1: the caller picks from
one day's list and is told another day. It is worth closing on the record,
because the replay output will keep pointing at it.

**A first scan claimed 12 occurrences in 11 calls, across all four clinics and
as recently as 5 September. That scan was wrong.** It carried a stale
`offer_day` across unrelated lookups, so a clean call — `CA7454c983a1`,
northgate, where the caller moved off Saturday of their own accord and picked
Tuesday correctly — was reported as a defect.

Scoring only the strict adjacency (numbered offer → caller answer → the very
next bot block), and comparing against the offer's **full** weekday set:

```
calls 921   mismatched confirmations 3   in 3 calls
```

and one of those three is the caller's own words — *"let's go back to that
first friday one"* — so the Friday confirmation is right.

The remaining two are `CAb81fe6519` and `CAec93b0320`, both `jv_v1`, both with a
NULL `build_sha` (pre build-stamping), both offering dates in the **first week
of August**. The suppression that fixes them — `v3_slot_phrase_superseded`, and
the CALL STATE block that reads it — landed on **2 August** in `100b5614` and
`7b698f63`, and its comment names `CAb81fe651` as the call it was written for.

**Zero occurrences after those two, over 921 calls, through 8 September, on all
four clinics.** Closed. Do not re-open it from the replay output.

### The lesson, since this is the third time

A corpus scanner that carries state between calls will manufacture defects. The
first pass reported 12 and four of the five it named as recent were clean. Both
scans took ten minutes to write; only the second was worth anything. **Bound the
adjacency before believing a count** — and read the transcripts of every hit,
not a sample, when the total is small enough to allow it. Here it was, and
reading them is what turned 12 into 2.
