## F-1 · The slot readout — ROOT CAUSE FOUND · `BLOCKER`

**Observed, every single call:**

| Call | Options in one breath |
|---|---|
| 3 · 17:59 | 6 |
| 5 · 18:35 | 6 |
| **4 · 18:29** | **9** — *"Number 1 … quarter to seven, half past seven, or quarter past eight. Number 2 … half past four, quarter past five, six, or quarter to seven. Number 3 …"* |

B1 was marked ✅ CLOSED by `de426a6`. **It never took effect, and here is why.**

`de426a6` edited step 5 of `clinic_template_prompt.py`, which does now say exactly
the right thing — *"Offer exactly TWO times… TWO — not three, not six… as ONE
natural sentence with no numbered list."* And `jv_v1` does use that prompt
(`prompt_engine = "template_v1"`, confirmed in `clinic.json`).

**But the slot presentation is not generated by that prompt.** At
`llm_stream.py:995–1010`, the moment `check_availability` returns, the system
swaps model and prompt:

```python
from app.prompts.susie_system_prompt import SLOT_FORMATTER_SYSTEM_PROMPT
model = HAIKU
_call_system = SLOT_FORMATTER_SYSTEM_PROMPT
```

That focused ~1.5K formatter prompt is a **deliberate latency optimisation** — the
full 19K persona prompt caused a ~9 s cold prefill on Haiku. And it mandates the
opposite behaviour:

> *"3 days: 'Here's what we've got coming up — Number 1, [day_label] — [times].
> Number 2 … Number 3 … Any of those suit you?'"*
> *"The numbered format is mandatory for any presentation of 2 or more day options."*

The transcripts match that template **verbatim, word for word**. The persona
prompt's two-slot rule is never in context when slots are spoken.

### Why the obvious fix is wrong

Do **not** simply delete the numbering. The formatter says why:

> *"Keep the 'Number 1, [day_label] — [times]' structure completely unchanged
> (it is parsed for keypad selection)."*

Stripping the numbers breaks keypad slot selection.

### The correct fix — cap the data, not the wording

`receptionist_tools.py:2353`:

```python
_present_days = days_data[:3] if _presentation_mode == "multi_day" else days_data
```

Cap to **two** days in `multi_day`, and trim each presented day to **one** time.
That yields two options, keeps the numbered wording keypad-parsing depends on, and
is deterministic rather than a plea to a model.

Critically, `session["available_days"]` (set at line 2296) still holds the **full**
set, and `_resolve_slot_iso` reads from it (line 777) — so a caller can still pick
a time that wasn't read out. Nothing is lost, only the readout shortens.

**Note:** call 4 produced 3 and 4 times on single days, exceeding even the
formatter's own "up to TWO times" rule. A data-side cap fixes that too; a prompt
edit would not have.

## F-3 · The "phantom booking" is a transcript artifact — CORRECTION

Call 6, turn 27 reads *"All booked — you're in for Friday the 31st…"* with no
calendar event. That looks like F-023. **It almost certainly is not.**

Two code paths carry the model's words:

```
llm_stream.py:1497   chunk = sanitise_response(chunk, session)   → TTS   ← Gate 5f runs HERE
llm_stream.py:1109   full_reply += chunk_text                    → raw, ungated
llm_stream.py:619    _append_history(session, user_text, full_reply)   → obs transcript
```

The comment at line 624 states it: *"full_reply is assembled from raw."*
**The obs transcript is what the model generated, not what the caller heard.**

Gate 5f verified working against the exact string:

```
_false_confirmation_claim("All booked — you're in for Friday the 31st …") → True
```

`booking_write_confirmed` was never set on that call, so the guard's precondition
held. It fired and returned the re-steer question. Corroborated by the caller's
next turn — *"yes book it in"* answers a question, not *"we'll see you then."*

> ### Implication beyond this call
> **Every "All booked" quoted from an obs transcript in the findings docs carries
> this artifact** — including the 26 Jul verify call that reopened F-023. If those
> quotes came from transcripts rather than from what the tester heard, F-023 has
> been over-diagnosed. Confirm before spending time on it.
>
> Fix worth making regardless: capture `_false_confirm_guard_fired` into the obs
> record, so "did the caller hear a phantom" becomes a query instead of a debate.

**This is `29e3f9b` working exactly as designed** — armed on the symptom

`clinic.json`'s own `_escalation_note` documents this exact class of defect being

## F-8 · Silent time substitution — caller asked for 17:30, she booked 17:05 · `P1` · **NEW**

**Call 6 · 18:38 · `CAa4942bcea4` · turns 6 → 7.**

Turn 5 offered Friday 31st at *"half past four in the afternoon or **five past

Turn 6, caller: *"the last day that you offered at **half past five** in the

The caller asked for a time that did not exist. Rather than flag the mismatch,

## F-10 · `collected` cannot audit the two things that matter most · `P1 observability` · **NEW**

The `collected` blob is identical in shape on all eight calls:

```json
{"name": …, "phone": …, "reason": …, "chosen_day": null, "selected_slot": …}
```

- **No `service` field.** F-021 (wrong service booked) is reproducible 4/4 and is
  the highest-severity open defect in the system — and **it cannot be checked from
  obs at all.** On call 6 she opened with *"what's the acupuncture for?"* and the
  caller then asked for an initial assessment; which service actually got booked
  is unknowable from the record.
- **`reason` is `None` on every non-booked call** (2, 3, 4, 5) even where the
  caller clearly stated it — "my left ankle's in a lot of pain", "a niggle from
  the gym". It is written only at booking time. So the field exists precisely when
  you don't need it, and is empty on every call you want to diagnose. It also
  makes RS-02 (fabricated reason) un-auditable.
- **`reason` conflates two things.** Call 6 stores a clinical reason (`'ankle pain
  from over walking and a slip'`); call 7 stores a *service* (`'acupuncture'`).
  Same field, different semantics.
- **`chosen_day` is `null` on all eight calls**, including both successful
  bookings. Dead field.

## F-11 · Chunk-join drops the space between sentences · `P2` · **NEW**

Present in **every** call that reached availability, and in three separate places
on call 7:

```
"…while I check what's available.Here's what we've got coming up — …"
"Let me get that booked in for you now.Shall I go ahead and book that in?"
"…let me do that now.So that's Quentin, Friday the 31st…"
```

Two chunks are concatenated with no separator. Also call 5 turn 7 carries literal
newlines: `"Right —\n\nWhat's the appointment for?"`. `sanitise_response` does
`result.replace("\n", " ")` — but *after* the Gate 5f block, and per-chunk, so a
chunk boundary escapes it.

Cosmetic in text; in TTS it removes the sentence pause, which is part of why the
slot readout sounds rushed.

## Demo script constraints these calls establish

1. Name a **specific day** — never "as soon as possible" or "anytime next week".
2. **Accept the caller-ID number.** Never ask to use a different one.
3. **Never touch the keypad.**
4. Give a **simple name, clearly**, with no "that would be…" lead-in.
5. Name **one** service, never two (F-021 remains open).
6. State the reason plainly (RS-02 deferred — reason can be model-supplied).
