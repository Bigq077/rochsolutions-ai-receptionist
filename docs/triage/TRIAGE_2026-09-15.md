## Findings

### 1. `final_state` is unusable — data defect, severity 4

All **929** stored rows report `final_state = GREETING`, including the 46
completed bookings. The column is never advanced past its initial value.

Anything that reasons about "where did the call stop" from this field is wrong.
The triage collector's stall table is therefore empty of signal on obs data —
use `turn_traces` or the transcript instead. Worth fixing at source, because it
is the natural first thing to reach for.

### 2. Vital Edge: 48 calls, 0 bookings — not a defect

Looks alarming, is not. **37 of 48 calls have zero turns**, median duration 5 s,
34 under 10 s. Nobody is speaking to Susie — these are hang-ups before the
conversation starts. The one long call ends with her saying *"Hi there — yes,
still here."*

Consistent with Vital Edge being on voicemail / a number not yet promoted.
**No engine action.** Worth confirming the number routing is what you intend.

### 3. Joint Venture carries 80% of real traffic

359 of 449 real calls, against 41 for Theorem. If JV has not formally gone live,
that volume needs explaining — and it means JV, not Theorem, is where engine
quality is actually being exercised. `abandoned` is 255 of 359 (71%), against
17 of 41 (41%) at Theorem.

## Not clustered

51 calls carry findings with no judge tag and no derived signal
("unclassified"). They are counted, not diagnosed. Most are likely short
abandons; worth a pass if the abandon rate matters.

## Caveats

- **The `booking_confirmed` ↔ id match only proves the two agree**, not that the
  appointment is correct. Wrong-service or wrong-time bookings would still show
  as clean here; they are caught by `wrong_info` / the judge, not by this check.
- Operator test calls are **52%** of all rows (480 of 929). Any fleet statistic
  that does not exclude them describes the tester.
- `slot_offers` is NULL before 2026-09-03 and `build_sha` before 2026-07-31, so
  build-level attribution over this window is partial.
